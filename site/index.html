Minerva

The effective price of an auction lot, right next to the hammer price: converted into your currency, with the buyer's premium, VAT, shipping and payment costs included.

What it does

On a supported auction site, Minerva reads each price and its currency, converts it into the currency you pay in, adds the costs you entered in its side panel - auction premium, VAT on the hammer price, shipping split over the lots you expect to win, import VAT, payment costs and flat fees - and shows the result right after the original price. It stays up to date while the bids change.

The costs are saved per auction, so every auction house and site keeps its own. A click on a result shows how the price adds up, part by part. The panel also shows the exchange rate the page needs.

Supported sites: biddr.com, numisbids.com and l5.com.

Install

Chrome / Edge

  1. Download and unzip the Chrome package.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  3. Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Click the toolbar icon to open the side panel.

Firefox

  1. Download the Firefox package.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Load Temporary Add-on… and select the zip.
  4. Click the toolbar icon to toggle the sidebar.

Temporary add-ons are removed when Firefox restarts.
